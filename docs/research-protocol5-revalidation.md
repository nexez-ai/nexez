# Protocol-5 recovery design

The owner approved correcting qualification and uniformly revalidating all
5,000 provisional protocol-4 observations on September 23, 2026. One cumulative
USD 100 ceiling and the original October 12 deadline remain. A reservation is
not a provider charge. No publication or outreach is authorized.

## Separate observations, one corrected protocol

Keep protocol 4 paused and immutable for audit. Create a separate protocol-5
revalidation root containing exactly the 5,000 predecessor result targets.
Each target links to its predecessor result, preserves its original domain,
URL, category, region, source reference and sampling rank, and starts with a
fresh bounded scan. Do not copy a score or relabel an old observation. All
corrected metrics come from the corresponding new qualified response. Recheck
failures are exclusions, not zero scores, and do not prove the historical page
was invalid. Persist only metrics and closed diagnostics, never response bodies.

The root must be frozen only after exact predecessor-result coverage is checked.
The predecessor remains stopped, with its cost counters protected after linking.
The new base reserve must carry the complete prior USD 43.025 reservation rounded
up to USD 43.03. The prospective USD 0.0027 rate is supported by the documented
60-second resource model and separate non-compute reserve, not a hard invoice cap.
Do not reduce old charges. An initial 500-success, 1,500-attempt, 500-dispatch
checkpoint reserves at most USD 44.38. Audit it before processing the remainder
of the revalidation frame. This is a targeted revalidation, not a new 75,000-site
pilot. All 5,000 must reach a terminal outcome before any continuation.

Seal a finite family ceiling of 120,000 new attempts and 20,000 new dispatches,
including this revalidation. At USD 0.0027 and the carried reserve this is at
most USD 97.03 before the continuation's upward carry rounding. Allowing one
additional cent gives USD 97.04, leaving USD 2.96 extra unallocated margin.
Active stage limits are much smaller. These ceilings do not guarantee 50,000 results or full-frame
completion. Lower yield, retries, changed usage and review pauses can reduce
the achieved sample. Intermediate gates and the original deadline still apply.

## Qualification correction

Retain the existing 80-character accessible-text minimum, MIME checks, robots,
SSRF, DNS pinning, byte/probe/time bounds and scorer version 2. Add closed,
research-only exclusions for unreadable decoding/unsupported encodings,
default server pages and directory indexes, empty JavaScript shells,
redirect-only interstitials and previously missed challenge/holding templates.
Use complete-body or bounded multi-indicator patterns so ordinary business
prose about hosting, domain sales or JavaScript does not become ineligible.
Legitimate short pages, business navigation, relocation, retirement and closure
statements with readable business content remain eligible. No JavaScript
execution, challenge bypass or decompression is introduced.

## Subsequent sampling and continuity

Only after the entire revalidation and its quality/cost gate pass may a
same-protocol continuation be prepared. It may include the other original
70,000 candidates and the existing 50,000-domain offline extension, excluding
all 5,000 revalidation initial domains. Previously failed or duplicate protocol-4
candidates are not silently declared protocol-5 exclusions; they remain eligible
for fresh protocol-5 processing within bounds. Preserve original sampling ranks
and frozen provenance, with an explicit derived-frame manifest and hashes.
The continuation importer/manifest is a separate gate, not activated here.

This prioritizes previously accepted candidates before remaining candidates.
A bounded partial result is therefore not a simple random prefix of the nested
125,000 frame. Disclose the two-phase order, recheck dates, changed protocol,
actual corrected denominator, remaining frame and all exclusions. More records
do not make it nationally representative or comparable to August.

Each new result uses the protocol-5 root identity namespace. At most one
same-protocol continuation and one active family wave are permitted. Verify
hash-salt continuity before sealing. Cross-wave final-domain deduplication and
shared cost carry must remain enforced. Superseded protocols 1-4 and smoke
never contribute observations to the corrected final dataset.
